## 1. Why OWND exists (the core problem)

Morocco's streetwear scene runs on imported hype: copies of US/EU designs, generic print-on-demand hoodies, and pages that sell a picture instead of a reason. A young Moroccan who wants to wear something that says *who they are* has two options: an expensive foreign brand that knows nothing about their life, or a cheap local copy that means nothing.

**OWND's answer:** every piece is a *chapter* in a story about taking control of your own life. You don't buy a hoodie. You buy a chapter and add it to your story.

### The question this whole document answers
> *"Why would someone buy from OWND instead of any other brand?"*

1. **Every piece has a written story.** A real story (founder, community member, or a Moroccan reality) is attached to each design, printed inside the garment and told on the website.
2. **The customer becomes an author.** Buyers "sign" their chapter and can submit their own story; the best ones become future designs. Clients don't just wear the brand, they help write it.
3. **Rooted in Morocco, built for the world.** Moroccan reality (Darija, cities, struggle, ambition) told in a universal language, the same way US brands export "American" stories worldwide.
4. **The keyhole.** The logo's keyhole = *unlock your own story.* It's a symbol people can own and repeat (hand sign, tag, patch), not just a wordmark.

## 2. Brand architecture

**Model: Branded House.** One masterbrand (OWND), with everything underneath it named consistently.

```
OWND (masterbrand: "Own your story")
├── CHAPTERS        → main collections, released as numbered drops (Chapter 01, 02, ...)
│     └── each piece = a "Page" with its own story, number, and limited run
├── BLANK PAGE      → core essentials (plain heavyweight hoodie/tee + keyhole only), always in stock, cash flow
├── WRITTEN BY YOU  → community line: designs built from stories customers submit
└── OWND STUDIO     → (month 12+) collabs with Moroccan artists, athletes, creators
```

**Why this structure:** Chapters create hype and urgency (limited). Blank Page creates steady revenue (always available). Written By You creates loyalty and free organic content. Studio creates reach for international growth.

## 3. Target audience (hypotheses, to be validated) [INTERVIEW]

### Primary persona: "The Builder" (hypothesis)
- 18–28, Casablanca / Rabat / Marrakech / Tangier / Agadir, plus the Moroccan diaspora (France, Belgium, Spain, NL, Italy)
- Student, freelancer, young entrepreneur, athlete, creator: someone *in the middle* of building their life
- Lives on Instagram and TikTok. Follows Corteiz, Represent, Essentials, Hellstar, and local street artists/rappers
- Pains: feels underestimated; people (family, society, the algorithm) try to define them
- Wants: to look clean and premium, and to wear something that *says something*, not just a logo

### Secondary persona: "The Diaspora Kid"
- 18–30, Moroccan origin living abroad, wants a link to home that looks modern rather than folkloric
- Higher purchasing power (EUR), and the natural bridge to going international

### Questions to validate
- Gender split (unisex by default?), exact age band, budget per piece (MAD), city distribution
- Who are their heroes? What do they listen to? What do they argue about online?

## 4. Brand identity system

### 4.1 Logo
- Black, liquid/melting wordmark. The **O is a keyhole**: the brand's signature symbol
- Usage to define: monochrome black/white, the keyhole as a standalone icon, minimum sizes, clear space
- Derived assets to create: keyhole icon, "Chapter" numbering system, inner-neck label, woven tag

### 4.2 Color (proposal)
| Role | Color | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Core | Ink Black `#0B0B0B` | The ink you write your story with |
| Core | Paper Off-White `#F2EFE8` | The blank page |
| Accent | one per Chapter (e.g. Chefchaouen blue, Sahara clay, Atlas green) | Each chapter gets its own color world |

### 4.3 Typography (proposal)
- Display: the logo carries the personality, so keep type quiet
- Text: one clean grotesk (e.g. Inter / Neue Haas style) plus one typewriter/mono font for "story" text (the written-chapter feel)

### 4.4 Voice
- Short, direct, confident, never corporate
- Mix of English (global) and Darija (local truth). Example: *"Own your story. Ma t3tich l9lam l chi had." (Don't hand the pen to anyone.)*
- Talks *to* the customer as an author, never as a "consumer"

### 4.5 Signature product details (what makes it feel OWND)
- Printed **story inside the hoodie** (inner back or inside the pocket)
- **Numbered** pieces: "Chapter 01 / Page 07 / 0042 of 150"
- Hang tag with a QR or NFC chip → opens that piece's story page, where the owner can register their name ("written by ___")
- Heavyweight fabric (400+ GSM), boxy fit: premium feel is non-negotiable

## 5. Product strategy [INTERVIEW]

### Launch (Chapter 01)
- 3–5 designs maximum, hoodies first (plus 1 tee for an entry price)
- Small runs (50–150 per design) → sell out → scarcity → story
- Blank Page essentials: 1 hoodie, 1 tee, 2–3 colors

### Pricing hypothesis (to validate)
| Item | Morocco (MAD) | International (EUR) |
|---|---|---|
| Chapter hoodie | 450–650 | 75–95 |
| Blank Page hoodie | 350–450 | 60–70 |
| Tee | 199–279 | 35–45 |

Target gross margin ≥ 60%. Production: Moroccan textile manufacturers (Casablanca / Tangier / Fès), which keeps "Made in Morocco" as part of the story.

### The story-per-piece process (repeatable for every design)
1. **Story**: one real story, ≤ 150 words (source: founder, community, Moroccan reality)
2. **Line**: one sentence that captures it (this goes on the garment)
3. **Symbol**: one visual metaphor (design)
4. **Proof**: one piece of content showing the real person or place behind it
5. **Release**: teaser → story reveal → drop → owners' photos

## 6. Go-to-market: organic-first (low ad spend)

Inspired by Want & Need-style, Corteiz-style guerrilla and community-driven US/UK brands.

### Pillars
1. **Story content** (40%): the story behind each piece, told in 15–40s videos (face, voice, place)
2. **Build in public** (25%): the founder's journey: sampling, factory visits, failures, numbers
3. **Community / "Written by you"** (20%): stitch and duet customer stories, owner features, street interviews ("What's the chapter you're writing right now?")
4. **Drops & hype** (15%): countdowns, secret passwords, location drops, waitlists

### Guerrilla tactics (zero/low budget)
- **Keyhole hunts**: hide sticker keyholes around cities; whoever finds the code gets early access
- **Password drops**: the site is locked (keyhole), and the password comes only through IG/TikTok and the waitlist
- **Seeding**: 10–20 pieces gifted to *micro*-creators (5k–50k) whose real story fits. No scripts, just their story
- **Pop-up "Story Booth"**: one-day pop-ups in Casa/Rabat where people record their story → future content
- **Waitlist over ads**: every channel pushes to the waitlist (WhatsApp + email)

### Channels
| Channel | Role |
|---|---|
| TikTok | Reach / discovery (story videos, build in public) |
| Instagram | Brand world, drops, community, DMs |
| Facebook | Older audience / parents gifting, Marketplace reach, retargeting later |
| WhatsApp | Customer service, COD confirmation, VIP broadcast list |
| Website | The home of the stories + store |

### Daily content system
- Claude prepares a daily idea pack (3 hooks, 1 script, captions in EN/FR/Darija, posting time)
- The founder films and posts. Weekly review: what worked → double down

## 7. Website (built from zero) [INTERVIEW]

### Principles
Clean, minimalist, mobile-first (90%+ of traffic will be phone), fast on Moroccan 4G, story-first.

### Sitemap (v1)
- **Home**: keyhole intro → current Chapter → manifesto → waitlist
- **Chapters**: each collection as a "book"; each product as a "page" with its story first and the product below
- **Product page**: story, photos, size guide (boxy fit), price, COD option, edition number
- **Written by You**: story submissions form + featured stories
- **Story lookup**: `/p/<code>`, the page a QR/NFC tag opens; the owner registers their name
- **About / Manifesto**, **FAQ / Shipping / Returns**, **Contact (WhatsApp)**
- **Locked drop mode**: password page for drop days

### Requirements
- Languages: EN + FR at launch, Arabic/Darija later
- Payments: **Cash on Delivery (essential in Morocco)** + card (CMI / Stripe-capable provider for international later)
- Shipping: Moroccan courier (e.g. Amana, Cathedis, Ozon Express); international in phase 3
- Waitlist and email/WhatsApp capture
- Analytics: traffic source, conversion, waitlist growth

### Build options (decision needed)
| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| A. Custom site (Next.js) + Shopify backend (headless) | Full design control, scales internationally | More build work |
| B. Shopify theme, custom-designed | Fast, reliable checkout, COD apps | Less unique |
| C. Fully custom (site + own orders/COD) | Cheapest monthly cost | You run all operations yourself |

**Recommendation:** start with B or a light version of A. Launch fast, then go custom once the brand has traction.

## 10. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Quality not premium enough | Sample 2–3 manufacturers, never launch below standard |
| COD refusals / returns (common in Morocco) | WhatsApp order confirmation, small deposit for high-value items, blacklist repeat refusers |
| Stories feel fake | Only real stories, show the real people, no invented "lore" |
| Copycats | Speed, community, and the stories themselves are the moat: a design can be copied, not a community |
| Founder burnout (content every day) | Batch filming 2×/week, reusable formats |
