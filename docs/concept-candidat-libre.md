# OWND — New Chapter 01 concept: "CANDIDAT LIBRE"

Date: 2026-10-06. Replaces the "MACHI KHDMA" idea after badr's feedback: the design must feel Moroccan, be something no Moroccan brand does, and make the community want it. Sketch: `brand/chapter-01-candidat-libre.png` (rough layout, not final art).

**Honesty note:** "no brand does it" means *I found none* in the brands and searches covered by `competitor-research.md`. Before printing, search Instagram and TikTok for "candidat libre", "n° d'examen" and "20/20" hoodie to confirm.

## The 3 concepts considered

| # | Concept | Idea | US/UK inspiration | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Hay" (neighbourhood chapters)** | Each Chapter is a real neighbourhood (hay/derb) of a Moroccan city. "Wach nta mn ina hay?" Numbered pieces, residents claim their hay, the community votes which one comes next. | City/borough/postcode pride merch (NYC, LA, London postcodes) | Needs your own hay to feel authentic; only appeals where the hay is known |
| 2 | **Arabizi cipher (3 / 7 / 9)** | Backs written only in Arabizi (the 3, 7, 9, 5 youth alphabet). Each Chapter hides a phrase people decode. | Hidden-code/password drops (Corteiz) | Arabizi is informal and unstandardised (spelling varies), and text-only designs need to be very well typeset |
| 3 | **CANDIDAT LIBRE (exam sheet)** | The hoodie back is a **Moroccan exam sheet**: candidate name box, exam number, subject, duration, and a **red-pen "20/20"**. Subject: "MA 9ISSA" (my story). Duration: "TOUTE LA VIE". | Number-as-identity (jersey numbers, "Class of" varsity), W&N's numbered drops | Seasonal feel (bac); needs a clean layout so it doesn't look like a joke tee |

## Recommendation: #3, with #2 as a small detail

**Why CANDIDAT LIBRE**
1. **Everyone in Morocco knows this exact page.** The exam header, the dotted name line, the red pen. It's a shared memory, not folklore, so people feel it instantly and tag friends ("this is us").
2. **It carries OWND's real story.** You finished the bac at 19 and "took the pen back". The page already has a pen (the examiner's) and a score; OWND flips it: *you* give yourself 20/20. (I'm using it as a metaphor only; I don't know whether you took the bac as "candidat libre", so don't claim that without checking.)
3. **Numbering is built in.** "N° D'EXAMEN: 0007 / 0050" is the piece number, written by hand by you two, the same idea already planned for the inside neck.
4. **It's ownable.** The red-pen "20/20" on a cream (paper) hoodie is a distinctive signature colour accent that none of the brands I checked use. Streetan, Taliri and W&N sell plain colours with logo or abstract graphics.
5. **It is a viral hook by itself.** "Hand in your paper" content: owners fill in the CANDIDAT line with a fabric pen and post their number.

**Layout (back print, 1 colour + 1 red accent)**
```
CANDIDAT(E) : ____________      <- owner writes their name
N° D'EXAMEN : 0007 / 0050       <- hand-numbered
ÉPREUVE     : MA 9ISSA          <- "my story" in Arabizi (the 9 = ق)
DURÉE       : TOUTE LA VIE
-------------------------------
        ( 20/20 )               <- red "teacher's pen", circled
CHAPTER 01 — CANDIDAT LIBRE
```
- Cream hoodie = exam paper. Black hoodie = same print in cream ink; the red circle stays red.
- Chest: small keyhole-door mark (already designed).
- Inside: the founder story (`chapter-01-kit.md`), unchanged.
- Cost note: a second ink colour (red) adds a screen. Ask workshops for the price of 1 vs 2 colours; fallback is a black-only "20/20" (loses the red-pen effect).

**Keep it from looking like a joke tee**
- Use the same typewriter font as the story, thin lines, lots of empty space.
- No government emblems, no ministry names, no real exam logos (avoids legal and tone problems).
- Only one red element.

## Community angle ("starving for it")

- **Scarcity with meaning:** 50 numbers. "Which N° d'examen are you?" Low numbers (0001–0010) have status.
- **Teaser content:** a masked candidate sits an "exam" on the street: question "ÉPREUVE: Ma 9issa. Durée: toute la vie." Answers from passers-by become content.
- **Bac results week (June):** natural anniversary drop and meme moment *(assumption: confirm timing; Chapter 01 can launch earlier)*.
- **Owner ritual:** write your name on the line and post "Candidat [name], N° 00XX, 20/20." The best posts get featured.
- **Next chapters keep the system:** Chapter 02 = "Rattrapage" (the resit: second chance), Chapter 03 = "Mention" (honours). Each is a real Moroccan exam word and a stage of the story, so the community can guess what comes next.
