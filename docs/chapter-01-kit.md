# OWND — Chapter 01 "The Pen": Kit

Date: 2026-10-06. Everything needed to make and announce Chapter 01. Builds on `chapter-01-playbook.md` (50 numbered hoodies, 499 MAD, cash on delivery, made in 3 batches, Paper + Ink colours, masked founders). Items marked *(assumption)* need checking with real workshops.

---

## 1. The story (printed inside every hoodie)

**Title:** Chapter 01 — The Pen

**Story (EN, ~125 words, under the PRD's 150 limit):**

> We were 14 when we decided to build a brand.
> Two cousins, one notebook, a logo drawn a hundred times.
> No money. No support.
> "Online business? That's not real work."
> So we stopped.
> Then we tried again. And stopped again.
> For five years, other people held the pen and wrote our story for us:
> what we should study, what counts as work, who we were allowed to become.
> At 19, bac in hand, we took the pen back.
> This hoodie is the first page we wrote ourselves.
> It's numbered because it's not a product, it's a page.
> And now it's yours.
> Whatever they told you that you can't do, write the next line anyway.
>
> **Ma t3tich l9lam l chi had.**
> Don't hand the pen to anyone.
>
> This page belongs to: ______________

**Short versions for captions**
- EN: "They held the pen for five years. At 19 we took it back. Chapter 01: The Pen."
- FR: « Pendant cinq ans, d'autres ont tenu le stylo. À 19 ans, on l'a repris. Chapitre 01 : The Pen. »
- Darija: "5 snin w nass kaykteb lina l9issa dyalna. F 19 3am, khdina l9lam. Chapter 01."

## 2. Design brief (send this to the designer/printer)

**Garment**
- Heavyweight hoodie, **400–450 GSM**, cotton fleece brushed inside (or heavy French terry), pre-shrunk.
- **Boxy fit**: dropped shoulders, wide body, slightly cropped length.
- Double-layer hood, **no drawstrings** (cleaner, more premium), kangaroo pocket, thick ribbed cuffs and hem.
- Colours: **Paper** (warm cream, target `#F2EFE8`) and **Ink** (black, `#0B0B0B`). Bring the hex codes and a printed swatch to the workshop.

**Prints** (screen print, one colour per hoodie to keep cost low)

| Placement | Content | Paper hoodie | Ink hoodie |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front, chest centre, small (~8 cm wide) | OWND wordmark (keyhole O) | black ink | cream ink |
| Back, large (~28 cm) | Keyhole symbol | black ink | cream ink |
| Back, under the keyhole | "MA T3TICH L9LAM L CHI HAD" in a typewriter font, then "CHAPTER 01 — THE PEN" | black ink | cream ink |
| Inside back (between the shoulders) | The full story from section 1, typewriter font, ~9 pt equivalent | black ink | cream ink |

**Numbering, done by hand (on-story and almost free)**
- Inside the neck: a plain woven or printed label "OWND — CH.01 / ____ OF 0050".
- The two of you **write each number by hand with a black or white fabric marker** (e.g. a laundry-proof textile pen) before packing. "The Pen" is literally the pen. Film it, because it's great content.
- Hang tag (card, keyhole die-cut if affordable): number again + QR code.

**QR code (no website needed yet)**
- Points to a free form "Register your page": number, name/Instagram handle, city, "what chapter are you writing right now?" (one sentence).
- Answers become owner features and future "Written by You" stories.

**Sizes and quantities**

| | S | M | L | XL | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper | 6 | 11 | 11 | 7 | 35 |
| Ink | 2 | 5 | 5 | 3 | 15 |

Batch 1 (15 pieces, paid from the budget): **10 Paper** (S2 M3 L3 XL2) + **5 Ink** (S1 M2 L1 XL1). Adjust batches 2–3 to what actually sells and what people reserve.

## 3. Workshop brief (for getting 2–3 quotes)

**Message to send** (WhatsApp, FR + Darija):

> Salam, on est une nouvelle marque de streetwear marocaine (OWND). On cherche un atelier pour un hoodie heavyweight 400–450 g, coupe boxy, en crème et noir, avec sérigraphie 1 couleur (devant, dos, intérieur). Première production 15 pièces, puis 2 réassorts (20 + 15). Est-ce que vous pouvez nous faire un échantillon et un devis ?
> Salam, 7na brand jdida dyal streetwear maghribiya. Bghina hoodie t9il 400–450 g, boxy, crème w k7el, b sérigraphie. Awel commande 15 pièces, mn b3d 20 w 15. Wach t9dro tdirou lina échantillon w devis?

**Questions to ask every workshop** (write the answers in one table to compare)
1. Minimum order per colour? Can they do **15 pieces** for the first run?
2. Price per hoodie at **15**, **50** and **100** pieces, with and without printing?
3. Exact fabric: composition (100% cotton? 80/20?), real GSM, brushed inside? Can they show a swatch?
4. Can they get a **cream/beige** fleece close to the swatch, and **the same dye lot for batches 2 and 3**? (Colour must match across batches, since all 50 are one edition.)
5. Sample: price and how many days?
6. Production time per batch after the sample is approved?
7. Printing in-house or with a partner? Price per print placement?
8. Pre-shrunk? How much shrinkage after a wash?
9. Can they sew our neck label (we supply it)?
10. Payment terms: how much upfront for each batch *(assumption: workshops often ask 30–50% upfront)*?

**Sample checklist** (before approving)
- Weight feels heavy; the hood stands up on its own
- Wash it once: colour, shrinkage, print cracking
- Boxy fit on both of you and one friend of a different size
- Cream looks warm, not yellow or grey, in daylight photos
- Inside print readable, not ghosting through to the outside

**Where to look:** workshops in Casablanca, Fès and Tangier (the PRD's "Made in Morocco" plan). Ask local brands' followers and fabric sellers which workshops do heavyweight fleece.

## 4. Week 1 content pack (7 posts, all masked, phone only)

Rules for every video: same black mask with the white keyhole; hook in the first 2 seconds; 10–25 seconds; voiceover in Darija with EN subtitles (FR in the caption); one call to action. Post on TikTok, then the same video to Reels and Shorts.

| Day | Format | Hook (first 2s) | Shots | Caption + CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Teaser | Black screen, a keyhole slowly lights up | Close-up of the logo drawn on paper; a hand pulls the paper away; two masked figures from behind | "Something is being written. Chapter 01." Follow to read it. |
| 2 | Skit: "Machi khdma" | Family member (unmasked or from behind): "Online business? Hadi machi khdma." | Dinner table from the cousins' point of view; cut to two masked figures at 3am sketching, laptop light on the masks | "They said it's not real work. We said: watch." Follow. |
| 3 | Story (voiceover) | "We were 14 when we drew this logo." | Old notebook (or recreate one), hands flipping pages, crossed-out drafts, today's logo | "Five years. Same notebook. Chapter 01 is coming." Follow + save. |
| 4 | Poll / community | "Beige or baby pink? You choose a future chapter." | Two fabric swatches held by masked hands, side by side, daylight | "Comment beige or pink 👇". Also a story poll on Instagram (this validates the colour gap). |
| 5 | Build in public | "Our first workshop visit." | Fabric rolls, hands feeling weight, machines; no faces of workers without permission | "400 g. Made in Morocco. 50 pieces only." Comment "LIST" to get on the waitlist. |
| 6 | Street interviews | Masked interviewer: "What chapter are you writing right now?" | 3–5 short answers from people in your city (ask permission, show only those who agree) | "Every story deserves a page." Tag someone writing theirs. |
| 7 | Manifesto | "Don't hand the pen to anyone." | Fast montage of the week + the story from section 1 as voiceover; end on the keyhole | "Chapter 01: The Pen. 50 numbered pages. Waitlist open, link in bio / DM 'LIST'." |

**Waitlist mechanics (free):** WhatsApp Business broadcast list + a free form (name, city, size, colour, Instagram). Auto-reply on WhatsApp and Instagram DMs when someone sends "LIST".

**Track each day:** views, follows, comments, and "LIST" messages. After week 1, the format with the most "LIST" messages gets 2 posts in week 2.

## 5. Media buying (later, not week 1)

- **0 MAD on ads before the drop.**
- After batch 1 sells: **500–1,000 MAD** on Meta **click-to-WhatsApp** ads, boosting only the best organic video.
- Audience: 18–24, your cities (Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier + home city), plus people who watched 50%+ of your videos.
- Goal: WhatsApp conversations that turn into confirmed COD orders. Judge it by cost per confirmed order, not likes or views. Stop any ad that doesn't produce orders within the first ~200 MAD *(assumption: tune once real numbers exist)*.
