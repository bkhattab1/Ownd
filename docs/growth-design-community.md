## 1. Warm the pages fast, without getting the accounts buried

### The rule that changes the plan
Reposting other people's viral videos is **not** a safe shortcut on Instagram. Instagram now judges originality over a rolling 30 days: if **most** of what an account posts is someone else's content, the account stops being recommended (no Explore, no suggested reels) and only existing followers see it. Watermarks, small crops and a credit in the caption don't count as original. Genuine commentary, remixing, reinterpretation, official repost tools and collaboration posts do. ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/), [Planoly](https://planoly.com/blog/instagram-updates-its-original-content-policy)). Reposting also carries copyright risk. I could not check TikTok's current rules (that search was cancelled), so verify them before relying on the same approach there.

So: **use viral videos as raw material for ideas and formats, not as the post itself.** Same niche, same speed, no penalty.

### The 4-part warm-up (first 14 days, 0 MAD)

| Part | What to do | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Teach the algorithm your niche (days 1–3)** | On each new account, only follow, watch fully, like and comment on streetwear, Moroccan fashion, football-culture and "build a brand" accounts. 20–30 minutes a day, no buying followers, no bots or automation. | Both apps recommend based on what the account watches. A clean niche feed means your first posts are shown to the right people. |
| **2. Remake the viral formats (days 3–14)** | Every day, save 3 viral videos from the niche (US streetwear skits, Moroccan football edits, brand-founder stories). Re-shoot the *format* with OWND's story and the masks: same hook structure, same pacing, your own scene, words and voiceover. | Proven structure + original footage = original content. This is what W&N does with skits and what Taliri did with the "chase sequence". |
| **3. Remix with commentary (2–3 per week)** | Use the platform's native remix/stitch/duet tools to react to a viral video **with your own take** (e.g. a streetwear founder's story → masked reaction "this is our year 14 → 19"). | Explicitly allowed by Instagram as meaningful transformation; native tools show attribution and bring the original audience to you. |
| **4. Borrow audiences with collabs (from day 7)** | Offer collab posts and gifted pieces to **micro-creators (5k–50k)** in the Moroccan streetwear/football niche whose story fits (PRD seeding plan). Comment genuinely under their posts first. | A collab post appears on both profiles and is the one repost method Instagram rewards. Micro-creators cost less than ads and bring Moroccan trust. |

**Weekly mix once warm:** 4 videos remade from viral formats, 2 remixes, 1 build-in-public, 1 owner/community post, with 70%+ of the 30-day posts clearly OWND-original.

**Check yourself:** Instagram has an "Account Status" tool to see if reach is restricted. If a post underperforms 3 days in a row, change the hook (first 2 seconds) before changing anything else.

### What I won't recommend
Buying followers, engagement pods, bots or auto-comment tools, and reposting others' videos as-is. They risk the accounts you need for a 5,000 MAD launch.

## 2. A unique design, rooted in Morocco

Uniqueness test for every idea: *if the logo were removed, could someone name the brand from the garment alone?* Three directions that pass for OWND (pick one for Chapter 01):

| # | Direction | Idea | Why it's ownable |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **"Machi → Khdma" (the correction)** | Back print: "MACHI KHDMA" (it's not work) with **MACHI struck through by hand**, leaving "KHDMA" (it's work). Typewriter font, one line. | It's the founders' real story in two words. Only OWND has this sentence. Works as a viral hook ("they said it's not work"). |
| **B** | **The keyhole is a Moroccan door** | Redraw the keyhole logo inside the **arched silhouette of a Moroccan door** (bab), drawn in single-line style. Back print large, tiny version on the chest. | Local architecture + the existing keyhole symbol. Doors = "opening your story." Not a US/UK look. |
| **C** | **Page grid** | Faint notebook-grid print across the back with handwritten crossed-out drafts of the logo (the 14-year-old's notebook), numbers in the margin. | Turns the notebook from the story into the pattern. Hard to copy because it's built from your own history. |

**My pick: A on the back + B as the small chest mark.** A carries the story and the hook; B carries the brand symbol. Hand-numbering inside is the third unique element.

**Make it Moroccan without folklore:** no generic zellige or caftan clichés. Use real Moroccan *life* (doors, Darija wording, notebooks, football, bac stories) in a modern minimal form, the way the PRD asks ("Moroccan reality told in a universal language").

**Check before you print:** I cannot confirm that no brand worldwide uses the same idea. Do a reverse image search and an Instagram/TikTok search of the phrase "machi khdma" and the door-keyhole before paying for screens. Registering the OWND name and logo with OMPIC is worth a later look *(assumption: check cost and process with them)*.

## 3. Community and marketing strategy: US/UK tactics adapted for Morocco

| Origin | What they did | OWND's Moroccan version (inspiration, not copy) |
|---|---|---|
| Wants and Needs | Waitlist → live → "almost gone" → next number; skits | **WhatsApp broadcast list** (Moroccans live on WhatsApp) instead of email; skits in Darija about "machi khdma" situations; each drop is a Chapter number. |
| Corteiz | Password site, secret location events, no ads, community swaps | **Keyhole hunt**: hide sticker keyholes in Casa/Rabat/Marrakech; the code is a WhatsApp word that unlocks the password page. A "Story Swap": bring an old hoodie that has a story, trade it for a discounted Page. |
| Broken Planet | TikTok-first growth, tiny raffles (15 pcs) | **"Page 0001" free draw**: comment or send your chapter to enter; one winner per drop. Costs one hoodie. |
| Drop (Casa) | DM-only sales, tiny runs, each drop funds the next | Same: **cash on delivery, batches, WhatsApp orders** (already in the playbook). |
| Trikko | Music-led content (rappers in studio) | **Collab with a Moroccan rapper or football content creator** for a Chapter: their real story on the inside of a limited piece. |
| Represent | Founder grind story | Your 14 → 19 story is the equivalent; keep telling it. |

### Community structure (build in this order)

1. **Waitlist = a WhatsApp broadcast list** (people reply "LIST"); free, personal, high open rates *(assumption)*.
2. **Close circle: first 50 owners** get a WhatsApp group ("The Library") where they register their number, vote on Chapter 02 colours, and submit stories. Owner perks: first access, their name in the next Chapter's credits.
3. **Written By You:** the best submitted story becomes part of a future design (already in the PRD). Announce it publicly so people want to be chosen.
4. **Status, not discounts:** low page numbers (0001–0010) have status; owners who post a photo with the number get featured. No sales or coupons; the scarcity is the product.
5. **Moments, not posts:** tie drops to Moroccan moments: **bac results (June)**, Eid/Ramadan weeks, major football matches, and summer when the Moroccan diaspora is home *(assumption: check the calendar for Chapter 02 timing)*.

### Rules for the community
- Real stories only, shown with permission.
- Never reveal the founders' faces unless you decide to.
- Answer every DM and comment in the first hour on drop day.
- Never promise a date you can't keep (batch timing is an estimate).
